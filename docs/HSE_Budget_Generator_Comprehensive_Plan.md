# OpsLix HSE Project Budget Generator — Comprehensive Planning Document

## Purpose

A complete budget tool that supports BOTH:
1. **Tender preparation** — what to estimate when bidding (risk-priced, with documented basis)
2. **Post-tender execution** — what to actually spend, month by month, with cash flow

Built for **global construction context** — India, UAE, UK, KSA, Qatar, Singapore — with jurisdiction-aware statutory items.

---

## Project Lifecycle — 4 Phases of Cost Incidence

Every line item in the budget should be tagged with WHEN it's incurred. This drives cash flow and tender pricing.

| Phase | When | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **TENDER** | Pre-award (cost of bidding) | HSE plan prep, site visits, insurance quotes, prequalification |
| **MOBILIZATION** | Award → Work start | Statutory deposits, license fees, infra construction, mass PPE issue, HSE recruitment |
| **EXECUTION** | Work in progress (recurring + one-time) | Salaries, replacement PPE, training, audits, mock drills |
| **CLOSURE** | Demobilization → Defects | Final medicals, decontamination, restoration, close-out report |

A 19-month project might show: 1 month tender, 2 months mobilization, 14 months execution, 2 months closure.

## The 19 Recommended Categories (Final)

Splitting old "Communication & Awareness" into 3 dedicated categories and adding Tender + Mobilization phases as their own categories. This gives full lifecycle coverage.

### 1. Tender & Pre-Award Costs
Costs incurred to PREPARE the bid (recoverable only if you win).
- HSE plan preparation (in-house staff time × hours OR external consultancy fees)
- Site reconnaissance / risk walks for HIRA development
- Initial HIRA, JSA, Method Statement drafts for tender submission
- Pre-qualification documentation (ISO certs, training records, performance history)
- Insurance quotation acquisition fees
- Subcontractor pre-qualification screening
- Statutory clearance feasibility / scoping fees
- Client HSE plan review meetings (travel, time)
- Technical proposal HSE chapter writing
- Tender HSE clarification responses

### 2. Mobilization & Statutory Clearances
One-time costs at project start (before work commences).
- **BOCW Welfare Cess deposit (India)** — 1% of project value, MAJOR LINE ITEM
- Workmen's Compensation insurance premium (period-based)
- Contractors All Risk (CAR) insurance
- Public Liability insurance
- Employer's Liability insurance (UK: £5M min)
- Professional Indemnity (designer scope)
- Environmental Impairment Liability
- BOCW registration (India), Factory Act registration if applicable
- Pollution Control Board NOC, MoEF clearance, Forest clearance
- Fire department NOC (state/jurisdiction)
- Building plan sanction fees
- Boiler license, Lift license, Explosives license (if applicable)
- ESI/EPF registration of HSE personnel
- Trade licenses (state-specific)
- Iqama/visa fees per worker (KSA, UAE, Qatar)
- Wage Protection System enrollment (UAE WPS)

### 3. HSE Personnel
Recurring monthly cost over project duration.
- HSE Manager (per BOCW Sec.36 thresholds: >250 workers = mandatory)
- HSE Officers (typically 1 per 100-250 workers)
- Safety Stewards / Marshals (1 per 50 workers)
- Fire & Emergency Officer
- Environmental Officer
- Occupational Health Doctor (full-time / part-time / on-call)
- Paramedic / Nurse (24×7 for major sites)
- HSE Trainers (in-house dedicated)
- HSE Documentation Officer
- Behavior-Based Safety (BBS) Coordinator
- Joining bonuses, relocation, accommodation, transport
- Background checks (criminal verification)
- Pre-employment medicals

## Cross-Cutting Considerations

### Risk-Based Pricing (for tender stage)
Some line items should be priced based on RISK, not just unit cost:
- Specialty PPE for high-risk activities — over-provision is cheap insurance
- Emergency equipment — should always exceed minimum
- Training time — under-allocation leads to incidents

### Cash Flow Profile
- Mobilization category = front-loaded (months 1-3)
- HSE Personnel = flat monthly (entire duration)
- Training = front-loaded plus periodic refresher waves
- PPE = initial spike + steady replacement
- Audits = periodic (Q1, Q3 typically for surveillance)
- Closure = back-loaded (final 2 months)

### Industry Benchmarks (% of project value)
- **Light construction / commercial buildings**: 0.8-1.5%
- **Heavy civil / highway / bridge**: 1.5-3.5%
- **Oil & gas / petrochemical**: 3.5-6.5%
- **Tunneling / underground**: 4.0-7.0%
- **Power / hazardous chemicals**: 3.0-5.5%
- **Marine / offshore**: 4.5-8.0%

### Multi-Jurisdiction Variations
| Item | India | UAE | UK | KSA | Qatar | Singapore |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Statutory cess | BOCW 1% | None | CITB ~0.35% | None | None | SDL 0.25% |
| Mandatory crèche | Yes (>50 women) | No | Optional | No | No | No |
| Heat stress mandatory ban | No | June-Sep summer | No | June-Sep midday | June-Aug | No |
| Visitor induction | Required | Required | Required (CDM) | Required | Required | Required |
| Mock drill frequency | Quarterly | Quarterly | 6-monthly | Quarterly | Quarterly | Annual |
| Fire NOC | State PCB | Civil Defense | Building Control | Civil Defense | Civil Defense | SCDF |
